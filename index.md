# Camera Import – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 4, 2026_

Camera Import does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- **No data collection.** The app has no accounts, analytics, tracking, or ads, and makes no network connections.
- **Photos.** Photos you choose are read from the folder or camera you select and saved to your Photo Library on your device. Photos never leave your device through the app.
- **Photo Library access.** The app asks for add-only access so it can save your photos. It cannot read your existing library.
- **Local settings.** The app remembers the last folder you picked, and only on your device. Deleting the app removes it.
